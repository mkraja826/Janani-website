Reminders

Remember what your care plan already asks you to do.

Mothers can save a medicine name, timing, duration and recurring schedule so PregaLove can help them remember. PregaLove organizes reminders; it does not prescribe medicines or change treatment.

Medicine nameSave the medicine exactly as provided.
Timing & durationKeep the existing schedule organized.
Recurring schedulesSupport for repeated routines.

Notification supportSurface reminders when they are due.
Clear boundariesNo prescribing, diagnosis or medicine changes.
Wellness & nutrition

Personalized support should still know its limits.

PregaLove can personalize supportive wellness information using the pregnancy stage and information the mother voluntarily provides, such as height, weight, preferences or relevant health details.
