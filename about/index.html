About PregaLove

Hundreds of small pregnancy moments. One calmer place for them.

Pregnancy creates questions, responsibilities, emotions, routines and memories. PregaLove exists to bring those pieces together into a supportive experience that feels modern, private and human.

Mission

Help every mother feel more informed, organized, supported and connected during pregnancy.

Why PregaLove

Technology should make pregnancy feel less fragmented.

Instead of forcing mothers to jump between notes, reminder apps, search results and family chats, PregaLove is designed to bring supportive pregnancy organization into one coherent experience.

Product principles

The boundaries are part of the product.

PregaLove should earn trust through what it chooses not to do as much as through the features it offers.

01

Mother First

Design around the comfort, clarity and needs of expecting mothers.

02

Privacy by Design

Collect information only when needed for the intended experience and handle it responsibly.

03

Family With Consent

Family experiences should respect the mother's privacy and choices.

04

Technology With Boundaries

AI can assist with information but should never pretend to replace qualified healthcare professionals.

05

Accessibility

Work toward supporting mothers across languages, regions and levels of digital familiarity.

Medical Safety

PregaLove supports pregnancy organization and understanding. It does not replace medical care.

PregaLove provides pregnancy organization, reminders, educational information and supportive wellness experiences. PregaLove does not provide medical diagnosis or emergency medical services.

Users should contact a qualified healthcare professional regarding medical conditions, symptoms, medications, pregnancy complications or treatment decisions.

For emergencies, users should immediately contact local emergency services or visit the nearest appropriate healthcare facility.

This language is intentionally calm and direct: PregaLove can support the journey, while qualified healthcare professionals remain responsible for medical assessment and treatment.

Responsible AI

Useful assistance should never become false medical authority.

PregaLove AI is intended to simplify information and help users navigate their PregaLove experience. Safety design should prevent it from being presented as a doctor, diagnostic engine or emergency service.

Appropriate role

  • Explain pregnancy information in simpler language
  • Help navigate PregaLove features
  • Organize supportive, non-diagnostic information
  • Encourage professional care when a medical concern needs it

Outside PregaLove's role

  • Diagnosing pregnancy complications
  • Changing medication or treatment
  • Replacing prenatal appointments
  • Handling emergencies instead of local emergency services
