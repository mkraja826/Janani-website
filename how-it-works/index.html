How Janani works

A pregnancy journey that feels simple from the first day.

Janani brings the practical pieces of pregnancy together without asking mothers to become experts in apps, health systems or technology.

1
Create your Janani journey

Begin with the basics.

The mother creates her account and provides basic pregnancy information needed to shape the Janani experience.

2
Personalize the experience

Make Janani relevant to this pregnancy.

Content can adapt to pregnancy stage and information the mother voluntarily chooses to provide.

3
Organize daily pregnancy life

Keep routines in one calm place.

Add medicines, schedules, reminders and useful information that already belongs to the mother's care routine.

4
Understand your journey

Follow milestones without information overload.

Use pregnancy-stage and maternal wellness information to understand what part of the journey you are in.

5
Stay connected

Invite support without giving away control.

Partner and family experiences can help loved ones understand how to be supportive while respecting the mother's privacy choices.

6
Keep the memories

Save the moments you may want later.

Use the journal to keep pregnancy memories, reflections and milestones together in a personal space.

Clear boundaries

Support should be useful without pretending to be healthcare.

Janani is designed to complement, not replace, the qualified professionals and services responsible for medical care.

What Janani does

  • Helps organize pregnancy routines and reminders
  • Provides educational pregnancy and wellness information
  • Supports journaling and pregnancy memories
  • Helps families understand how to be more supportive
  • Uses technology to simplify the Janani experience

What Janani doesn't do

  • Replace obstetricians or gynecologists
  • Provide emergency medical services
  • Replace hospitals or laboratory testing
  • Diagnose medical conditions
  • Prescribe or change medicines or treatment
The idea is simple

Organize what matters. Explain what helps. Keep the mother in control.

That principle guides how Janani approaches reminders, personalization, family features, AI and privacy.

Calm by design.Janani should reduce pregnancy-related digital clutter, not create more of it.
