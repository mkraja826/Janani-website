How the model works

Sustainability first. Then a transparent share of surplus.

The final commercial percentage will be set after Janani has enough real operating data to make a sustainable commitment. We will not advertise a fixed percentage until it can be supported consistently.

1. Commercial revenue

Janani subscription revenue is first reconciled with app-store fees, refunds, taxes and other payment adjustments.

2. Essential operating costs

AI, database, storage, hosting, notifications, security, support, compliance, accounting and essential product maintenance are deducted.

3. Sustainability reserve

Janani may retain an appropriate operating reserve so the pregnancy-support service can continue safely even if revenue or costs change.

4. Distributable surplus

The committed share of eligible distributable surplus is allocated to verified child-welfare organisations under the published Giving policy.

5. Human approval

Transfers are not autonomously released by software. An authorised review confirms the organisation, amount, banking identity and supporting records before payment.

6. Public reconciliation

Only completed and reconciled donations are published. Failed transfers, proposals and unverified claims never count toward the public total.

Important distinction

A Care+ purchase is not a charitable donation by the subscriber.

A user pays for Janani services through the applicable app store. Janani Giving is a separate allocation made later by Janani from eligible distributable surplus. This separation keeps refunds, taxation, app-store billing and NGO accounting clear.
