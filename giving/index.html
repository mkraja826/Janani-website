Giving philosophy

Build something sustainable first. Share eligible success transparently.

PregaLove will not advertise a fixed giving percentage until real operating data supports a commitment that can be maintained responsibly.

1. Commercial revenue

Revenue is first reconciled with app-store fees, refunds, taxes and other payment adjustments.

2. Essential operating costs

AI, database, storage, hosting, notifications, security, support, compliance and essential product maintenance are accounted for.

3. Sustainable operation

PregaLove may retain an appropriate reserve so the pregnancy-support service can continue safely as revenue and costs change.

4. Eligible surplus

Only eligible distributable surplus can be considered for the Giving program under the published policy.

5. Human approval

Organizations, amounts, banking identity and supporting records should be reviewed before any transfer is released.

6. Public reconciliation

Only completed, verified and reconciled contributions belong in the public transparency record.

What PregaLove may support

Practical needs that can help children and families thrive.

Potential areas may include child nutrition, education, essential clothing, maternal and child welfare, and support for vulnerable families. Final recipient organizations must be verified before PregaLove publishes any contribution.

Transparency Principle

No invisible promises.

The long-term vision is to make eligible giving visible and auditable through the PregaLove website wherever possible while keeping bank details, private health data and internal sensitive records protected.
