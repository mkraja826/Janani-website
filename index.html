Designed around today

Useful support without turning pregnancy into a dashboard.

Janani is being built as a companion first: important care is surfaced when needed, while deeper tools stay organized and easy to reach.

⏰

Care & medicine reminders

Create reminders from the instructions you already received from your maternity-care team, with persistent Android notification support.

📖

Pregnancy journal

Keep memories and reflections private by default, with explicit sharing choices for a linked partner.

📈

Health tracking

Organize weight, blood pressure and other user-entered health information without presenting Janani as a diagnostic tool.

♡

Partner connection

A support-first partner experience, including family linking and small “Thinking of you” moments.

✨

Janani Care+

Personalized supportive information with strict safety boundaries. Care+ does not diagnose, prescribe or change medicines.
