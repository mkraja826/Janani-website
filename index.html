A family philosophy

Pregnancy is experienced by one body, but it should never feel like a journey taken alone.

PregaLove is designed around the mother first, while helping the people she trusts become more thoughtful participants in the pregnancy journey.

Support, not surveillance.Family experiences are designed around consent and do not automatically expose the mother’s private PregaLove space.
For mothers

A softer place to organize the practical and remember the meaningful.

Routines, milestones, journal moments and pregnancy information can live together without making the experience feel clinical.

For families

Better support begins with understanding.

Partners can learn about pregnancy stages, notice important moments and receive useful context about how to be supportive—while the mother remains in control of what is shared.

Privacy matters

Your pregnancy journey belongs to you.

PregaLove is designed with privacy and responsible data handling in mind. Personal pregnancy information should be collected only when it supports the intended experience, and family features should respect the mother’s choices.
