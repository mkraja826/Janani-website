Account control

Delete your PregaLove account

The safest and fastest deletion method is the authenticated control inside PregaLove. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Permanent action: deleting a mother account also removes its associated family pregnancy space and dependent shared records. Deleting a partner account preserves the mother’s family pregnancy space.

Delete while signed in

  1. Open PregaLove and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings and scroll to Delete account.
  3. Enter the account’s current password and type DELETE exactly in the confirmation field.
  4. Select Delete account permanently, read the final warning and confirm.

The request is submitted only from an authenticated session. If PregaLove reports an ordinary error, the account has not been confirmed as deleted.

What deletion removes

Mother account: removes the account, associated family pregnancy space and records that depend on that family.

Partner account: removes the partner account and data that depends on it while preserving the mother’s family pregnancy space.

On-device data: sign-out and deletion cleanup is app controlled. Clearing PregaLove app storage or uninstalling removes app-controlled local data from that device.

Infrastructure providers may retain limited backups or security logs for periods governed by their operational or legal requirements. See the Privacy Policy.

Delete without reinstalling PregaLove

If you no longer have the app, PregaLove currently provides a separate secure static deletion page. Check the address carefully before entering credentials.

Open secure account-deletion page

The secure page sends credentials only to Supabase Auth and PregaLove’s protected deletion function. Do not enter credentials on a lookalike domain.
