For families

Pregnancy happens to a mother. The journey belongs to a family.

PregaLove is designed to help partners understand more, notice more and support more—without turning the mother's private pregnancy space into a shared dashboard.

The Partner Experience

A separate view built around support, not access.

Partners can receive useful pregnancy context and thoughtful prompts without automatically seeing everything the mother records in PregaLove. Sharing should follow the mother's consent and choices.

Small prompts, better support

Sometimes a tiny reminder changes the whole tone of a day.

The partner experience can use simple language to turn pregnancy information into something useful and human.

A gentle thought
“Today she may need more rest.”
A small action
“A small reminder can mean a lot.”
A shared milestone
“Another week of your journey together is complete.”
Thinking of You

A small moment that says: I remembered you.

PregaLove's “Thinking of you” concept is designed for tasteful, low-pressure connection between partners—small encouragement rather than noisy social features.

Privacy by relationship

Being family does not mean seeing everything.

A dedicated partner-oriented experience can be useful without exposing the mother's entire private app. PregaLove's direction is to make sharing intentional, understandable and revocable wherever practical.

Family support

PregaLove's long-term philosophy is to build healthier support systems around expecting mothers.

Technology cannot replace care from people, but it can help the people around a mother understand when their attention, patience and presence may matter.

Mother first.The family experience exists to support her journey, not take control of it.
