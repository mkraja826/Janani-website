PregaLove Support

Get help without exposing pregnancy, account or security-sensitive information.

Do not post sensitive information publicly. Never publish passwords, family invite codes, pregnancy or health information, medication details, journal text, Care+ conversations, access tokens or screenshots containing personal data.

Public technical support

Before the dedicated private commercial support mailbox is launched, non-sensitive technical issues may be reported through PregaLove’s public GitHub issue tracker. Describe the screen, action attempted, expected result, actual result, device model and Android version. Remove personal information from screenshots and logs.

Open public technical support

Private commercial support

A monitored PregaLove support mailbox will be published here before paid commercial launch. PregaLove will not publish a personal email address as the permanent customer-support contact.

Account deletion

For permanent account removal, follow the account-deletion instructions. Do not send account passwords through support.

Medical concerns

PregaLove support cannot provide emergency or individualized medical advice. For an urgent or medical concern, contact local emergency services or a qualified maternity-care professional.
